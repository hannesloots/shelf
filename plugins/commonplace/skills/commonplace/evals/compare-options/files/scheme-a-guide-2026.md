# Scheme A — Essential Hospital Plan: Benefit Guide 2026

## Contributions (effective 1 January 2026)
Main member: R 2 450 per month. Adult dependant: R 2 100. Child dependant: R 780.

## Hospital cover
Unlimited hospital cover at 100% of the Scheme Rate in any private hospital. Co-payment of R 3 500 applies to admissions not pre-authorised.

## Day-to-day benefits
No day-to-day benefit. GP visits are paid from your own pocket, except Prescribed Minimum Benefit conditions.

## Chronic cover
Chronic medicine for the 26 Chronic Disease List conditions only, at a designated pharmacy.
