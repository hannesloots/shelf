# Scheme B — Core Saver Plan: Benefit Guide 2026

## Contributions (effective 1 January 2026)
Main member: R 3 120 per month, of which R 620 is allocated to your Medical Savings Account. Adult dependant: R 2 640. Child dependant: R 940.

## Hospital cover
Unlimited hospital cover at 200% of the Scheme Rate in network hospitals; 100% outside the network. Pre-authorisation required; a co-payment of R 5 000 applies for non-network admissions.

## Day-to-day benefits
Day-to-day claims, including GP visits at R 520 per consultation, are paid from the Medical Savings Account (R 7 440 per year for a main member) until it is depleted.

## Chronic cover
Chronic medicine for the 26 Chronic Disease List conditions plus 12 additional conditions.
