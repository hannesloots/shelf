# Mavundla v MEC: Department of Co-operative Government and Traditional Affairs KwaZulu-Natal (extract)

[24] The applicant's heads of argument cited seven cases.

[25] On enquiry, counsel conceded that the authorities had been generated by an artificial intelligence tool.

[26] It emerged that counsel lazily omitted to verify the research produced by the tool, and that the cases did not exist.

[27] The duty not to mislead the court rests on every legal practitioner. A practitioner who files authorities without reading them breaches that duty.

[28] The conduct is referred to the Legal Practice Council for investigation.
